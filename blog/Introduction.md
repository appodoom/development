## Introducing the team

We are a dedicated team composed of four ambitious students, a distinguished professor of computer science from the **American University of Beirut** _(AUB)_, and an expert in derbakke performance. United by our shared passion for music, technology, and cultural heritage, we aim to explore innovative intersections between artificial intelligence and Eastern rhythmic traditions.

## General Overview

We are developing a **Large Derbakke Model** _(LDM)_, a specialized rhythm-aware transformer designed to deeply understand and represent percussive elements, with a particular emphasis on the derbakke, an iconic instrument in Eastern music traditions. Much like how large language models have revolutionized text-based creativity, communication, and understanding, the _LDM_ aims to accurately model complex rhythmic patterns, subtle variations, and expressive nuances found in percussion. We are trying to capture diverse percussion performances, our model seeks to generate rhythmically coherent, culturally authentic, and creatively inspiring patterns, facilitating innovative interactions between human performers and AI-generated percussion content.

## Visions and Hopes

Our vision is that the _Large Derbakke Model_ becomes a great helping tool for percussionists, especially Derbakke specialists, empowering their musical creativity in unprecedented ways. We foresee musicians collaborating interactively with the _LDM_ to discover new rhythmic motifs, refine compositional ideas, and enhance their improvisational capabilities. Beyond individual creativity, our broader ambition is that this technology will significantly advance the theoretical understanding of Eastern rhythmic traditions, potentially uncovering new frameworks within Eastern music theory itself. We hope the _LDM_ will not only enrich contemporary artistic expression but also contribute meaningfully to the preservation, evolution, and global appreciation of Eastern percussion traditions, bridging cultural heritage with cutting-edge AI.
